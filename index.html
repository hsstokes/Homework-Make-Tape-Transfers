You Need

🎞️Clear Sellotape
🖨️Laser-printed images
✂️Scissors or knife
🥣Bowl of water
🧽Towel or sponge
Laser printer only. Inkjet prints will not work.

How It Works

1 · Stick
Put tape on your printed image.
2 · Rub
Rub the tape hard. No air bubbles.
3 · Soak
Put it in water for a few minutes.
4 · Peel
Rub off the paper. The image stays on the tape.

Ideas to try

Texture
Use textures from your project.
Overlap
Layer transfers for depth.
Text
Add words to your design.
